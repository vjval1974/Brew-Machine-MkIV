About Max

Max Goold is a 13-year-old competitive surfer from Palm Beach on the Gold Coast, Queensland, who cut his teeth in the beach and point breaks of the southern Gold Coast around Currumbin Alley and Palm Beach. A consistent finalist and division winner across Surfing Australia's national junior pathway, Max announced himself nationally by taking out the U12 Boys Woolworths Surfer Groms Comp in Cronulla (Dec 2023), then backed it up with a national title in the U12 Grom Boys division of the 2024 Australian Junior Online Surf Championships.

In 2026 he stepped up to the U14 Boys ranks and immediately delivered back-to-back wins at Rounds 1 and 2 of the Surf Reflections Junior Series (Bonny Hills and Hungry Head), confirming his standing among the country's most promising junior surfers. Equally at home in punchy Gold Coast beach breaks and the cold-water NSW points of the Junior Series, Max combines a competitive heat brain with a progressive, rail-driven approach that suits modern junior surfing. He is actively seeking wetsuit and surf-apparel partners to support his 2026 national campaign.
